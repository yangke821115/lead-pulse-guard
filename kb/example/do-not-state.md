# Do not state

<!--
Injected into the prompt as prohibitions. Every line beginning with "- "
is a hard rule.
-->

- Do not quote a price in a public comment. Pricing depends on configuration; move the conversation to direct messages.
- Do not confirm or deny certifications unless the exact certificate number appears in confirmed-facts.md.
- Do not state specific technical parameters that are not listed in confirmed-facts.md.
- Do not compare our products to a named competitor.
- Do not promise a delivery date. Give the standard range and note that it is subject to written confirmation.
